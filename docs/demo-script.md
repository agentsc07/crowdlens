# 2-minute demo script (screen recording)

1. **0:00 to 0:12** Open the Space. Say: "CrowdLens answers plain-English questions about crowd video, locally, counting attributes, never identities." Show the footer with MOT17 attribution.
2. **0:12 to 0:30** Stage 1: pick sample *MOT17-04* (overhead Japanese street). Click Open. Play a few seconds of the raw video: "nobody counts this by eye."
3. **0:30 to 0:55** Stage 2: click the chip *wearing a red top* (cached, instant). Cut to Explore: stats (Yes / No / too small), evidence grid of thumbnails. Point at confidence numbers.
4. **0:55 to 1:15** Open the annotated video: green boxes only on matches, per-frame count in the HUD. Open *Over time* tab for the count chart.
5. **1:15 to 1:35** Back to Ask: type a movement question, *walking to the right*. Say: "answered from track geometry, no model call, instant."
6. **1:35 to 1:50** Switch to *MOT17-11* (mall). Type a question that is not a preset, e.g. *wearing a scarf*: point at the "Quick answer, unverified" warning and the ranked grid. Then ask *carrying a backpack* (cached, verified). Show Settings, drag the threshold from 0.9 to 0.5: "0.5 says yes too often, here is why we chose 0.9."
7. **1:50 to 2:00** Show the README results table: "F1 0.76, count error 34 %, search-then-verify halves cost at equal F1. Labels are Claude-made, human audit pending, and the 10 % goal is not met." End on privacy line.
